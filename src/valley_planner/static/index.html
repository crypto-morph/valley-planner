Sprinklers

On your farm: S a Sprinkler, Q a Quality Sprinkler, orange dots are tiles still to hoe, a cross is where another Scarecrow goes. The plan below is the same, one square a tile.

Getting them in

  • Line the plan up on your Scarecrow, marked X.
  • A Sprinkler won't go on a tile with a crop on it, so place them on empty dirt as crops come up, by the last day of the season.
  • One placed today waters from tomorrow morning. The crops around it still need the can today.
  • Keep the regrowing crops planted over the change of season, because bare dirt mostly disappears on day 1.
  • Tiles hoed today start dry. Seeds sown on dirt that was already there get watered next morning.
  • You can walk through normal crops, so leave no paths. Only Hops, Grapes and Green Beans block you.

What to cook

Worth cooking now

Which quality to cook with

The gain from each dish if its ingredients are all of one quality. A dish has no quality of its own, so better ingredients only raise what you give up: where it's red, sell that quality and cook with the plain ones. Cooking takes from the end of your backpack first, then the fridge, not the cheapest, so carry only the quality you mean to use.

Your other recipes

Ingredients are costed at their best other use (sold, or through one of your machines), or today's shop price if you don't have them.
